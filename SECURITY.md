# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Write to suport@cadex.ro with what you found and how to reproduce it. Please do not open a
public issue for it, and do not attach real CGXML files: they hold personal data.

## In scope

The CADex plugin as published on plugins.qgis.org and in this repository, in its latest
version: what it does with the files it reads (CGXML, ZIP archives, Excel and CSV tables,
layers) and with the files it writes.

## Out of scope

QGIS itself and its libraries, and the cadex.ro website.
