# Icon Attribution

All icons in this folder are original CADex artwork,
(c) 2026 RYXLAB SOFT TECH SRL (RyxLabs), licensed under
the plugin's license (GPL-2.0-or-later, see ../LICENSE).

`settings.svg` and `help.svg` are the Vernier gear and question mark,
recolored. Vernier is by the same author.

`cadex.svg` is the CADex mark from cadex.ro, rewritten by
`scripts/build_brand_icon.py` into the SVG subset Qt can render.

The palette comes from the cadex.ro design tokens. Two variants ship:
`icons/` for light themes and `icons/dark/`, generated by
`scripts/build_icon_themes.py` - edit the light icons, then regenerate.
