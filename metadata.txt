about=Tools for Romanian systematic cadastre work with CGXML files (ANCPI / OCPI, eTerra). The interface is in Romanian.

    * CGXML conversion: the .cgxml files of a folder, a ZIP archive or a file selection become an imobile (property) layer and a construcții (building) layer, as GeoPackage, Shapefile, DXF with labels or Excel. The columns written, their order and their field names are configurable; Excel headers use the readable column names. Owners and possessors come from property, possession and provisional registrations; every other person in the file is listed in a separate column, with their right or registration. Holes are cut out of the imobil and islands inside holes become separate parts; eTerra exports and deliveries are read with the same rules. From a folder only the .cgxml files are read, and a damaged ZIP archive is reported while the other sources are converted.
    * Intravilan / extravilan report: the area of every imobil inside and outside the intravilan and its status, as a classified layer and an Excel report with one sheet per status, statistics and unidentified owners.
    * Area reduction: scales the deed areas of a parcel list proportionally so that they add up to the measured contour area. Parcels with a CF/IE (land book number or electronic identifier) keep their area. Total rows and rows hidden in Excel are left out.
    * Comparator: two layers matched on a key column, or two sets of CGXML files (folders, ZIP archives or files) paired by file name or by IE. Lists the imobile and construcții added, removed and changed, split by what changed (boundary, data, both, split imobil, boundary not compared), with an optional Excel report.
    * Topology check: checks cadastral sectors for overlaps or gaps between imobile, unsnapped points and construcții outside their imobil. The data is not modified; the problems are returned as styled layers and an optional Excel file.
    * Building topology: cuts every construcție that crosses the boundary of its imobil back inside it, part by part, into a new layer that records the area removed.

    All computations and results are in Stereo 70 (EPSG:3844). Layers in other coordinate systems are reprojected on the fly, and a layer without a coordinate system is read as Stereo 70. A layer is refused when its coordinates do not fit its coordinate system (degrees in a projected system or without a coordinate system, metres in a geographic one) or, transformed to Stereo 70, do not all lie in Romania.

    Every tool is also an algorithm in the Processing Toolbox, under CADex, for models, batch processing and scripts. The help panel of each algorithm describes its parameters.

    Layers and fields are preselected by name patterns that can be changed in the settings. Several layers can be used for one role, for example imobile split by sector.

    The website cadex.ro, by the same author, validates CGXML deliveries against the ANCPI rules, runs predefined reports and compares two deliveries in full, persons and land book entries included. It is free for a single file.

    External dependencies: none for most tools. DXF output requires the Python package ezdxf. The plugin can install it into a folder of the QGIS user profile, after asking, without modifying the QGIS installation. It can also be installed with "python -m pip install ezdxf" from the OSGeo4W Shell.
